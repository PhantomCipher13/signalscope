Reliability Assessment

Scientific Limitations
  • Trained on CIFAKE (Stable Diffusion v1.4 only). Results may not generalise to DALL-E, Midjourney, Firefly, or other generators.
  • The calibrated probability is a statistical estimate, not a deterministic label. Values near 50% indicate high uncertainty.
  • Temperature calibration (T=2.876) reduces overconfidence on validation data — it does not guarantee real-world accuracy.
  • Robustness probes test stability under JPEG/resize transforms, not generator diversity.
  • Do not use this system as the sole basis for consequential decisions.
